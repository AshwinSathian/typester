Typester runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no cookies, and it sends nothing about you or your games anywhere.

What's stored, and where

Your settings (theme, motion, sound), your best score for each mode, your Daily scores for the last 60 days, your 40 most recent runs, your day streak and a count of hits and misses for each letter are saved in your browser's localStorage for this site. That data:

  • stays on this device and in this browser;
  • is not tied to any account or identity;
  • can be removed with "Clear scores" in Settings, or all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Network requests

After the app loads, Typester makes no network requests of its own. The word list ships with the app, which is also why it works offline once installed. Sharing a result goes through your device's own share sheet or clipboard, and only when you press Share.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles the requests your browser makes to load the site; Typester adds no tracking of its own on top.

Children's privacy

Typester collects no personal information from anyone, children included.
